# Problem Statement

The problem to solve is the lack of a trustworthy, near real-time way for consumers to compare the current performance of fast food stores in a specific local area. Existing platforms provide delayed, historical, and often unstructured review data, which is not sufficient for immediate decision-making.

Store performance can change rapidly depending on staffing, queue volume, time of day, and operational load. Because of this, historical reviews are often a poor indicator of the experience a customer is likely to have right now.

The solution must prioritise recentness of data, ease of comparison across stores, clear performance signals for key operational metrics, and visible confidence indicators based on the amount of recent feedback.
