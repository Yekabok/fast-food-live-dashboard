# User Personas

## Persona 1: Quick Decision Buyer
### Summary
A customer who wants to choose the best nearby fast food option right now with minimal effort.

### Needs
- compare nearby stores quickly
- see which store is currently performing best
- avoid poor service or inaccurate orders
- trust that the information is recent enough to matter

### Behaviours
- often mobile
- making a decision in transit or while hungry
- unlikely to read long-form reviews
- values speed and clarity over detail

## Persona 2: Active Contributor
### Summary
A user willing to sign in and contribute structured ratings after or during a visit.

### Needs
- easy sign-in
- fast contribution flow
- visible recognition for participation
- confidence that their input affects live results

### Behaviours
- may contribute repeatedly across different visits
- responsive to lightweight gamification such as badges
- willing to give structured feedback if the process is quick

## Persona 3: Trend Watcher
### Summary
A user interested in patterns over time, not just the current snapshot.

### Needs
- compare store performance across longer periods
- understand whether a store is improving or declining
- see more than a single live moment

### Behaviours
- more analytical than the average visitor
- likely to return to the platform repeatedly
- interested in charts, trends, and consistency over time
