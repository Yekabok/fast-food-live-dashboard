# New Zealand Legal and Privacy Notes

## Confirmed areas requiring attention
- Defamation risk relating to publication of ratings about identifiable businesses
- Privacy Act 2020 compliance for user account and contribution data
- Potential Fair Trading Act risk if methodology or confidence is presented misleadingly
- Harmful Digital Communications Act relevance if free-text comments target individuals
- Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act relevance for future marketing communications

## Primary-source findings captured so far
### Defamation Act 1992
- A body corporate may bring defamation proceedings if it proves pecuniary loss or likely pecuniary loss.
- Truth and honest opinion defences exist, but product wording and methodology matter.

### Privacy Act 2020
- Personal information collection, notice, storage, access, correction, retention, and overseas disclosure obligations apply.
- Privacy breach notification obligations may apply where serious harm is likely.

### Product implication
The platform should present results as recent customer-rated sentiment rather than official performance measurement.

## Recommendation
Before public launch, obtain New Zealand legal review of:
- product wording
- scoring methodology explanation
- privacy policy
- terms of use
- moderation and takedown process
